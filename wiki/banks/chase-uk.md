## Summary

An incumbent building a challenger. JPMorgan launched Chase in the UK on **10x SuperCore**,
a cloud-native core from a British vendor — the highest-profile proof that a tier-one bank
will rent its core rather than build it.

## Why it matters to this research

Three things:

1. **Cloud-native core is now acceptable to a tier-one risk function.** That removes the
   "regulators won't allow it" objection from any build-vs-buy conversation.
2. **10x's differentiators are migration tooling and a polyglot runtime** `[reported]` — the
   two things that matter when the buyer has an existing book, which is exactly JPMorgan's
   situation and exactly *not* Zopa's.
3. **An incumbent-backed challenger changes the competitive maths for UK neobanks.** Chase UK
   does not need the neobank's unit economics to survive.
