## Summary

The European Archetype B: full German banking licence, **bought core (Mambu)**, everything above
it in-house on AWS. Kotlin/Java microservices, a documented migration from a HashiCorp stack to
Kubernetes, and one of the better public post-mortems in neobanking.

in Docker. Useful as the *low* end of the neobank service-count range — against Monzo's >3,000
and Starling's ~20 at a similar age, N26's 60 sits in the middle and none of the three is wrong.

## The NLB war story — worth reading in full

N26's best engineering post documents a long-tail latency hunt `[confirmed]`. Symptom: HTTP calls
timing out against a 1-second client timeout, despite typical responses under 30ms.

Root cause: **when a new node joins an AWS Network Load Balancer its initial state is "healthy"
until proven otherwise by health checks.** New general-purpose worker nodes were therefore
receiving TCP SYN packets before they had passed a health check — and before Traefik was running
on them. Packets went to machines with no ingress controller. The 1-second symptom came from the
Linux kernel's hardcoded initial TCP retransmission timeout (RFC 6298).

Fix: a Kubernetes Service annotation added in 1.19 —
`service.beta.kubernetes.io/aws-load-balancer-target-node-labels: workload-type=traefik` —
restricting NLB targets to the nodes actually running the ingress controller.

Diagnosis used tcpdump, Wireshark, VPC flow logs and NLB target-group metrics. Two lessons
generalise: **a managed load balancer's default health semantics can be optimistic in a way that
silently breaks you at scale**, and long-tail latency in a Kubernetes estate is often a routing
question rather than an application one.

## What it tells the research

N26 is the cleanest EU counterpart to Zopa and Varo — licence held, core rented, engineering
spent on the layers above. Notably, **Mambu appears nowhere in N26's own engineering writing**;
the attribution comes entirely from Mambu's side. That asymmetry is normal and is exactly the
caveat recorded in [Vendor map — who uses what](../vendors/vendor-map.md).
