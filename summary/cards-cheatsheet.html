Three events, not one — and they post differently

Real time · ~2s scheme timeout

1 · Authorisation

No money moves. The issuer says yes or no and rings a hold against available balance. Your fraud score, limit checks and balance read all happen inside this window.

Post it as a hold — pending, not settled.

Usually T+1

2 · Clearing

The merchant submits the real amount. It can differ from the authorisation — tips, fuel, partial shipment, currency conversion.

Post the actual and release the hold. Ledgers that assume auth == clearing break here.

Net, per cycle

3 · Settlement

Money actually moves, net, between issuer and acquirer through the scheme’s settlement bank.

Reconcile against the scheme file, not against your own postings. This is where breaks surface.

A licence does not give you a BIN — separate applications, separate bodies, separate timelines, and unlike a sort code there is no halfway house. Four traps. A slow balance read means the scheme approves on your behalf with your checks skipped. Auth ≠ clearing — tips and conversion move the amount. Provisional credit is a contingent position, real money on a case you have not won. And the 8-digit BIN has been the standard since 2022 — systems that still parse six are silently mis-routing.
