The two things that decide your design

Direction

Credit-push cannot debit anyone

FPS, CHAPS, RTP, FedNow and SEPA Inst are push-only. They cannot pull funds, so none of them can carry a subscription, a repayment or any collection you initiate.

For that you need Bacs Direct Debit in the UK, ACH debit in the US or SEPA SDD in the EU — and every one of those brings a long reversal tail with it.

Exposure

Speed and finality move together

The instant rails are final on receipt — there is no recall, which is exactly why scam interception has to happen in flight.

The slow rails reverse for weeks or months. A Bacs Direct Debit indemnity claim is effectively unbounded. Your ledger must hold a contingent position for the whole window, not until the payment clears.

Access — Faster Payments only

“Direct” describes the connection, not the settlement

DCSP — directly connected and settling, with its own account at the Bank of England.

DCNSP — directly connected, but a sponsoring DCSP settles for it.

Indirect Agency — reaches the rail through a DCSP or DCNSP.

FPS terms only. Bacs has just Direct and Indirect Participant — no non-settling tier. CHAPS adds Indirect Access Provider.

The sort code advantage

You can hold your own sort code while still being an indirect participant. Customers get permanent account details from day one, and you can go direct later without a single detail changing.

There is no card equivalent — you cannot own a BIN without being a scheme principal member.
